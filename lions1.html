Welcome to the Detroit Lions Official FanPage!

The Detroit Lions are a professional American football team based in Detroit, Michigan. They are part of the NFC North in the NFL.

The Detroit Lions have a rich history in professional football, dating back to 1928 when they were known as the Spartans in Portsmouth, Ohio. They joined the NFL in 1930 and were relocated to Detroit in 1934, where they were renamed the Lions. The team has won four NFL championships, with victories in 1935, 1952, 1953, and 1957.

The Lions have also had several notable players, including Bobby Layne, Earl "Dutch" Clark, and Doak Walker. The team has a long-standing tradition of playing on Thanksgiving Day, a practice that began in their first season in Detroit. Despite their historical success, the Lions have not played in a Super Bowl and have only won one playoff game since their last championship in 1991. The Lions continue to be a significant part of the Detroit sports culture and have a dedicated fan base.
